AI X-Ray review / Medical AI

Welcome to OrthoX, an academic medical AI prototype for structured X-ray review and injury-description support. The app brings together visual review cues, anatomical context, safety checks, explainability, and evidence-based management context in one workflow.

Choose the review path that matches the information you have:

  • Use X-ray review when you want to upload or inspect an image-based case.
  • Use Problem Description when you only have written symptoms or injury context.
  • Use every result as decision support only; final interpretation remains with a clinician.

Not a diagnosis. Not a treatment plan. Human clinical review is required.
