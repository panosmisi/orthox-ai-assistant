Safety boundary

What this tool does not decide

This academic prototype does not provide a diagnosis, treatment plan, medication instruction, discharge decision, weight-bearing decision, immobilisation decision, surgery recommendation, or follow-up clearance.

It presents AI-generated review cues and evidence context for clinician review. Human clinical verification remains required.

Always review the image

The original X-ray and full study remain the primary material.

Always use clinical context

Symptoms, examination, mechanism, and local protocol are outside the AI output.

Never treat as all-clear

No retained cue must not be interpreted as fracture exclusion.
